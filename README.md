# Open Lode

A September 2026 briefing on Australia’s open critical minerals projects, read by Leo — an English gentleman.

Country briefings and element specials — each element by its name and symbol — live at `public/podcast/episodes/<slug>/briefing.mp3`. The long essays are Australia through Myanmar, plus rhenium (Re) and tantalum (Ta). The shelf beside them is the Survey’s 2025 mine line for every other critical mineral, and for every other country that mines one.

Figures are compiled from public reports (the major-projects list, Austrade’s prospectus, and company statements). They are a reading list, not advice.
